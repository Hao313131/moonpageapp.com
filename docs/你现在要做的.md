## 1 ⭐ 提交 Educational App Store（15 分钟，最重要）

**为什么是它**：我实测过的唯一一个「有公开提交表单 + 免费 + 分类对口」的目标。58 万注册家长/教师，独立评审，**1–3 天出结果**，不通过会给反馈、可以重提。

### 怎么做

1. 打开 **https://www.educationalappstore.com/developer**
2. 点 **"Submit your App"**（免费，原文 *"it's free and only takes a minute"*）
3. 按下面 7 栏**直接复制粘贴**
4. App Store 链接填 `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6788652725`
5. 素材/事实页填 `https://moonpageapp.com/press/`
6. 提交

### ① Title & one-line value prop

```
MoonPage — Cozy Bedtime Stories
Original illustrated bedtime stories, read aloud or in a parent's own voice.
```

### ② Short description

```
MoonPage is a bedtime story app for children aged 2 and up. It holds 45 original,
illustrated picture books that a child can hear from a professional narrator or in a
parent's own recorded voice. There are no ads, no third-party trackers and no account
to create.
```

### ③ Learning outcomes（EAS 要 3–6 条，这是最花时间的一栏，已写好）

```
1. Vocabulary exposure — each story is written in book language rather than
   conversational language, so a child hears words and sentence shapes that are
   unlikely to come up in everyday talk.

2. Narrative comprehension — every story has a clear shape (a character, a problem,
   a resolution), which supports a child's ability to follow and predict a sequence
   of events.

3. Listening comprehension and sustained attention — the app can be used entirely
   without a screen in the child's hands, supporting listening to a spoken story
   from beginning to end.

4. A predictable bedtime routine — stories are short by design (about five minutes
   each), which supports a repeatable wind-down sequence rather than an open-ended
   content feed.

5. Shared reading between adult and child — a parent can record their own reading
   of any story, so the app supports the adult-and-child reading relationship rather
   than replacing it.
```

⚠️ **不要改这栏的措辞。** 全部用 `supports` / `gives repeated exposure to` 这类描述性说法，**没有一句声称可测量的效果**。夸大的效果声明在独立评审里会被识破，而且我们**没有任何评测数据**。

### ④ Features / Benefits

```
Features
- 45 original illustrated picture books, written for ages 2+.
- Every story read by a professional narrator, or in a parent's own recorded voice.
- Stories download to the device and work with no internet connection.
- No ads. No third-party trackers. No account or login required.

Benefits
- Ends by design: each story is about five minutes, so bedtime has a natural finish
  rather than an endless feed.
- Adult stays in the loop: recording your own voice means the parent's reading is
  part of the experience, not replaced by it.
- Works away from home: offline playback covers travel, waiting rooms and power cuts.
- Nothing to configure: no account, no profile, no settings a child can get lost in.
```

## 3 Bing Webmaster Tools（2 分钟，**不用碰代码**）

**为什么**：Bing 的数据同时喂 **ChatGPT 和 Copilot**。而且 AI 引荐流量 2026 年在快速增长，而该赛道的头部站（storyberries）**主动封锁了 AI 爬虫**——这是我们相对它的结构性优势。

**怎么做**（有捷径，别去加 secret）：

1. 打开 **https://www.bing.com/webmasters**
2. 用你的 Google 账号登录
3. 点 **"Import from Google Search Console"**
4. 授权 → 它会自动列出你 GSC 里已验证的站点 → 勾选 `moonpageapp.com` → 导入

**导入 = 自动验证**，不需要 meta 标签、不需要改代码、不需要加 GitHub secret。（代码里其实已经预留了 `BING_VERIFICATION` 的通道，但走导入这条路更快。）

## 4 勾一个选项：那句「Trusted by thousands of moms」

**问题**：这句渲染在 **4 个索引页**（首页、`/bedtime-stories/`、`/cozy-bedtime-stories/`、`/bedtime-stories-app/`）。App Store 公开数据只有 **AU 4 条评分、US/GB 0 条**，App 上架才两个月——**我核实不到依据**。

**和上次那个 Google Play 承诺不同**：那个是可证伪的假事实（链接 404），这个是**无法证实的营销话术**（可能真有几千下载）。而且澳洲 ACCC 对误导性表述比美国 FTC 严。**所以我没擅自改。**

勾一个就行：

- ☐ **A** 换成可核实的具体数字（如真实的 App Store 评分表述）
- ☐ **B** 换成不可证伪的定性说法（例：`Made by parents, for the last twenty minutes of the day.`）
- ☐ **C** 保留 —— 如果你确实有下载量数据支撑
- ☐ **D** 4 处全删
