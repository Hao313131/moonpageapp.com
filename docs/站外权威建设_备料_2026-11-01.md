## 2. HARO / Qwoted / Terkel 专家引述（数字 PR 主线）

- 在 Help a Reporter / Qwoted / Terkel 设关键词警报：**bedtime routine, screen time toddlers, reading aloud, children's sleep, parenting apps, kids media**。
- 响应原则：30 分钟内发；开篇亮资历 + 给**可直引短句** + 给独家可核实数据。
- 可直引的事实（全部来自 /press，可核实，**不要夸大**）：
  - 45 original illustrated picture books, not public-domain reprints
  - each story ~5 minutes, designed to end (no endless feed)
  - no ads, no third-party trackers, no account required
  - a parent can record their own reading of any story
- 响应模板：
  ```
  Subject: Re: [记者选题]

  Hi [记者名],

  I'm [Hao], the developer of MoonPage, a bedtime story app for kids 2+.
  Happy to be quoted on [选题主题].

  A line you can use directly:
  "Most bedtime-story apps are ad-funded or ask a parent to make an account —
  we took the opposite path: no ads, no trackers, no login, and a parent can
  record their own voice reading any of the 45 original stories."

  For context: each story is ~5 minutes and designed to end, so bedtime has a
  natural finish rather than an endless feed.

  More facts and assets: https://moonpageapp.com/press/
  ```
- ⚠️ 拒绝买链 / 群发客座（踩 SpamBrain，2026 必惩）。
