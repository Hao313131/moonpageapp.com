## 1. 先说清楚一件反常识的事

**上一版报告把 Common Sense Media 当成"提交目标"，这是错的。**

实测：`commonsensemedia.org` **没有公开的 App 提交表单**。它的帮助页是 JS 渲染（抓下来只有 10 个词），`/app-reviews` 版块也没有 "submit" 入口。**它的评测是编辑独立选题**——只能 pitch 编辑，不能"提交"。

**凡是告诉你"去 Common Sense Media 提交你的 App"的 SEO 文章，都没实际打开过那个站。** 这条记下来，省得以后重复找。

同理，`appadvice.com` 抓取返回 502、`producthunt.com` 返回 403（反爬）——**这两个我没有验证成功，所以不放进清单**。没验证过的目标不写进来。

## 2. 🔴 P0：Educational App Store 提交材料（可直接复制粘贴）

**提交入口**：https://www.educationalappstore.com/developer
**费用**：**免费**（原文：*"Submit Share your app or tool - it's free and only takes a minute and get listed."*）
**免费 App 可以提交吗**：**可以**（原文：*"Can free apps be submitted? Yes - both free and paid apps are welcome."*）
**出结果**：**1–3 天**（快速通道）/ 10 个工作日（详细反馈通道）
**需要准备**：官方说 **30–45 分钟**。下面我已经替你起草好，你只需要核对 + 粘贴。
**不通过怎么办**：会给详细反馈，**改进后可重新提交**——所以先提交，不要等到完美。

### 2.1 Title & one-line value prop

```
MoonPage — Cozy Bedtime Stories
Original illustrated bedtime stories, read aloud or in a parent's own voice.
```

### 2.2 Short description（2–3 句）

```
MoonPage is a bedtime story app for children aged 2 and up. It holds 45 original,
illustrated picture books that a child can hear from a professional narrator or in a
parent's own recorded voice. There are no ads, no third-party trackers and no account
to create.
```

### 2.3 Learning outcomes（EAS 要求 3–6 条）

⚠️ **措辞红线**：下面每一条都用 `supports` / `gives repeated exposure to` 这类**描述性**措辞，**不声称可测量的学习效果**（不写 "improves reading scores" / "boosts literacy"）。夸大的 learning outcomes 在独立评审里会被当场识破，而且我们没有任何评测数据支撑。

```
1. Vocabulary exposure — each story is written in book language rather than
   conversational language, so a child hears words and sentence shapes that are
   unlikely to come up in everyday talk.

2. Narrative comprehension — every story has a clear shape (a character, a problem,
   a resolution), which supports a child's ability to follow and predict a sequence
   of events.

3. Listening comprehension and sustained attention — the app can be used entirely
   without a screen in the child's hands, supporting listening to a spoken story
   from beginning to end.

4. A predictable bedtime routine — stories are short by design (about five minutes
   each), which supports a repeatable wind-down sequence rather than an open-ended
   content feed.

5. Shared reading between adult and child — a parent can record their own reading
   of any story, so the app supports the adult-and-child reading relationship rather
   than replacing it.
```

**为什么这 5 条站得住**：它们全部描述**产品做了什么**（书语言、清晰叙事、可无屏使用、短篇设计、家长录音），而不是**宣称产生了什么效果**。EAS 的评审框架看的就是「教育价值 + 安全 + 实际影响」，描述功能比宣称效果更容易通过。

### 2.4 Features / Benefits

```
Features
- 45 original illustrated picture books, written for ages 2+.
- Every story read by a professional narrator, or in a parent's own recorded voice.
- Stories download to the device and work with no internet connection.
- No ads. No third-party trackers. No account or login required.

Benefits
- Ends by design: each story is about five minutes, so bedtime has a natural finish
  rather than an endless feed.
- Adult stays in the loop: recording your own voice means the parent's reading is
  part of the experience, not replaced by it.
- Works away from home: offline playback covers travel, waiting rooms and power cuts.
- Nothing to configure: no account, no profile, no settings a child can get lost in.
```

**AI Assessment**（EAS 表格里有这一栏）：MoonPage **不使用 AI 生成内容** —— 45 个故事全部是人工创作 + 人工绘制 + 人工录制的旁白。这一点如实填写即可，**在当下的评审语境里这是加分项，不是减分项**。

### 2.5 Ages / learner types / subjects & tags

```
Ages:            2–6 (early years / preschool)
Learner types:   children (early years); parents and carers (adult-guided use)
Subjects / tags: English / literacy · Reading · Storytelling · Bedtime routine ·

### 2.7 Data & safety summary

```
- No advertising of any kind.
- No third-party trackers or analytics SDKs.
- No account, login, email or profile required to use the app.
- Stories work offline once downloaded; no data is transmitted during reading.

⚠️ **不要写** "COPPA compliant" / "GDPR-K compliant" / "kidSAFE certified" —— **除非你真的通过过那些认证**。上面 5 条全部是**可现场核实的产品事实**（点开隐私政策就能对上）。

## 3. P1：Common Sense Media —— pitch 邮件（无提交表单）

**方式**：走站内 Contact 表单 / 编辑联系邮箱 pitch。**不要找"提交入口"，没有。**
**定位**：他们评测**家长最可能遇到的** App。我们的切入点是"无广告无追踪 + 家长录音 + 短篇设计"这三个可核实的差异点。

```
Subject: App for review — MoonPage (bedtime stories, no ads, no trackers)

Hello,

I make MoonPage, a bedtime story app for children aged 2 and up. I'm writing because
it may fit your apps coverage, and because two of its design choices are unusual in
this category:

1. No ads and no third-party trackers, and no account or login. (Many bedtime story
   apps in this space are ad-funded or require a parent profile.)
2. A parent can record their own reading of any story, so the app supports the
   adult-and-child reading relationship rather than replacing the adult's voice.

The other facts are deliberately boring: 45 original illustrated picture books (not
public-domain reprints), each about five minutes, narrated professionally or in the
parent's own voice, and playable offline once downloaded. Free to start; the full
library is a subscription through the App Store.

Every checkable fact, plus ready-to-use descriptions and brand assets, is on one
page so you don't have to email me back and forth:
https://moonpageapp.com/press/

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6788652725

Happy to send a promo code or answer anything factual.

[你的名字]
moonpageapp@gmail.com
```

**为什么这么写**：编辑每天收到大量 "please review my app"。这封邮件**先给两个可核实的差异点**，再给一个"所有事实在一页上"的链接——**降低对方的工作量**是 pitch 唯一有效的策略。

## 4. P1：Raising Children Network（澳洲 .org）

**为什么值钱**：澳洲官方育儿资源站，`.org` 高权重，且**你本人在澳洲**（这是一个真实的、可讲的本地关联）。
**方式**：走 contact / 资源建议渠道 pitch，**不是提交 App**。

```
Subject: Australian-made bedtime story app — resource suggestion

Hello,

I'm an Australian developer (based in Wollongong) and I make MoonPage, a bedtime
story app for children aged 2+. I'd like to suggest it as a resource on your pages
about bedtime routines and reading with young children, if you think it fits.

What might be relevant to your audience: the app has no ads, no third-party trackers
and no account requirement, stories are about five minutes each so they support a
predictable wind-down routine, and a parent can record their own reading of any story
rather than only hearing a synthetic narrator. It's free to start.

Facts and assets, all in one place: https://moonpageapp.com/press/

If it's not a fit, no problem at all — and if there's a preferred way to submit
resources, I'm happy to follow it.

[你的名字]
moonpageapp@gmail.com
```

⚠️ **不要请求"链接"**。请求**被考虑**。育儿 .org 站对"给我个外链"极其敏感，对"这是给家长的资源"才可能有反应。

## 5. P2：厂商自营 roundup（可投，但认清性质）

**2026-09-28 实测**，这类文章的共同点是**自己就是卖方**，在文章里列竞品是为了吃 "best bedtime story apps" 的搜索流量：

| 站点 | 文章 | 披露 |
|---|---|---|
| `bedtime-stories.fun` | *The Best Bedtime Story Apps for Kids in 2026 (We Tested 12)* | 文内自曝 **"Made by us Bedtime Stories (bedtime-stories.fun)"** |
| `storysplash.app` | *Best Bedtime Story Apps for Kids in 2026 — Tested by a Parent of Two* | 自己就是 StorySplash |

**判断**：它们**真的收录第三方 App**（文章里有 Vooks、Epic、Novel Effect、Readmio、Oscar Stories、Bedtimestory.ai），所以**可以投**。但预期要放低——外链价值中等，且它们未必愿意把版面给一个直接竞品。

**要投的话，钩子用差异点**：无广告 / 无追踪 / 家长录音 / 短篇有结尾。**不要用"我们的故事更好"**——它们不会为了一个竞品说自己产品差。

## 7. 必须诚实标注的两件事

1. **这些外链不会立刻改变排名。** 域名权重是慢变量，一两封 pitch 邮件不会让 94 个非品牌查询从 0 点击变成有排名。**它的作用是累积**，而累积需要时间。
2. **Educational App Store 我核实了"免费 + 有表单 + 接受免费 App + 接受故事类 App"，但没有核实"MoonPage 一定通过评审"。** 它的评审是独立的，会看教育价值。我起草的 §2.3 已经**刻意只用描述性措辞**来最大化通过率，但**不保证通过**。不通过会拿到详细反馈——那也是有用的。
